CrispAudio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

CrispAudio is an audio workstation for sound-effect synthesis, voice processing, and timeline editing. It is designed to be local-first and private.

Data we collect

None. CrispAudio does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Where your work is stored

Your sounds, projects, and settings are stored only on your device (in local app storage). They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Exporting or sharing a file is always an explicit action you take, to a location you choose.

Microphone

The Voice Processor can record audio using your device's microphone. The microphone is only accessed after you explicitly tap Record, and the recorded audio is processed entirely on your device. It is never sent to us or any server.

Text-to-speech (optional)

CrispAudio includes an optional text-to-speech feature that is off by default. It only works if you configure a speech-synthesis server address in Settings. If you do, the text you enter is sent to that server — one you choose and control — to generate audio. CrispAudio does not provide or operate such a server, and no text-to-speech data goes to us.

Children's privacy

CrispAudio does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
