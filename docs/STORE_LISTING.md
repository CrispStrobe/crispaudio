# CrispAudio — App Store listing

Source of truth for the App Store Connect metadata (iOS). Pushed via the ASC
API; field limits are checked by `scripts/check-store-listing.mjs`.

Positioning: a **sound-design studio for game makers and creators** — make
sound effects from scratch, turn voices into characters, and cut the result
into finished clips. Deliberately not "audio workstation" (generic) and not
transcription/TTS-first (that is CrisperWeaver's territory); speech is one
input to the sound-design workflow, not the product.

### Description
CrispAudio is a sound-design studio for the sounds your game, video or podcast is missing. Build an effect from raw oscillators, give a voice a new character, and cut the results into clean, ready-to-use files — all without leaving your iPhone or iPad.

SOUND EFFECTS FROM SCRATCH

• 16 generators for the sounds games need: coin pickups, lasers, explosions, power-ups, hits, jumps, UI clicks, alarms, portals, glitches, wind, drones and more.
• Every sound is synthesized, not sampled: square, sawtooth, sine and white, pink or brown noise, shaped by an ADSR envelope, FM, vibrato and an arpeggiator.
• An effects rack with distortion, bit crushing, chorus, flanger, ring modulation, delay and reverb.
• Randomize for a fresh idea, or Mutate to nudge only a few parameters of a sound you already like. Lock the parameters you want to keep.
• Two slots, A and B, with a morph slider between them — compare two versions and blend them.
• See what you hear: waveform, frequency spectrum, level meter and the envelope curve update as you play.

VOICES WITH CHARACTER

• Record with the microphone or open a recording, then transform it with eight voice presets: classic robot, deep robot, alien, cyborg, radio, metallic, demon and chipmunk.
• Pitch shifting that keeps the length of a line, time stretching, and formant shifting that changes the size of a voice independently of its pitch.
• Vocoder, filters, compressor, noise gate, tremolo and more, with instant A/B listening between the original and the processed take.
• Need a voice line you haven't recorded? Type it, pick one of the voices installed on your device, set speed and pitch, and drop it straight onto the timeline.

CUT IT INTO A FINISHED CLIP

• A multi-track waveform editor made for touch: cut, copy, split, reorder and snap clips to a grid.
• Fades with linear, exponential or S-curve shapes, automatic crossfades where clips overlap, and effects per clip.
• Open audio from the Files app, AirDrop or any app's share button — it lands on the timeline.
• Export as WAV (8, 16, 24 or 32-bit), MP3, AAC or Opus and send it anywhere with the share sheet: save to Files, AirDrop it to your Mac, or hand it to your game engine or video editor.
• Save whole projects, audio included, and pick up where you left off.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE

All synthesis, voice processing and speech rendering happen on your device. CrispAudio has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it works without an internet connection. The microphone is only used while you are recording.

Full undo and redo everywhere, light and dark themes, English and German.

CrispAudio is open source under the MIT licence.

• Dir fehlt noch ein Satz? Tippe ihn ein, wähle eine der Stimmen auf deinem Gerät, stelle Tempo und Tonhöhe ein und lege ihn direkt auf die Timeline.

ZUM FERTIGEN CLIP SCHNEIDEN

• Ein mehrspuriger Wellenform-Editor für die Touch-Bedienung: Clips schneiden, kopieren, teilen, umsortieren und am Raster ausrichten.

PRIVAT UND OFFLINE

Synthese, Stimmbearbeitung und Sprachausgabe laufen vollständig auf deinem Gerät. CrispAudio hat keine Konten, keine Werbung, keine Analyse und kein Tracking und funktioniert ohne Internetverbindung. Das Mikrofon wird nur während einer Aufnahme verwendet.

## App Review notes

No account or login is required, and the app works fully offline.

Suggested path: SFX tab → tap a generator (e.g. "Laser/Shoot") → press Mutate a few times → Export, which opens the share sheet. Voice tab → Record (microphone permission) or load an audio file → choose "Demon" or "Classic Robot". Timeline tab → "Text to Speech" → type a sentence → Generate → Add to Timeline (uses the on-device system voices, no network).

Audio files can also be sent to CrispAudio from the Files app via Share → CrispAudio.
